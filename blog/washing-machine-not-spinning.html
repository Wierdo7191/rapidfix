Washing Machine Not Spinning? Causes and Quick Fixes

7 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

Few things disrupt a laundry routine faster than opening your washing machine after a cycle and finding clothes sopping wet inside. When a washer is not spinning, the problem is rarely cosmetic. It points to mechanical or electrical faults that will not resolve themselves. In Nairobi, where many households rely on a single washing machine, knowing the difference between a quick fix and a serious repair can save you time and money.

This guide covers the most common reasons your washing machine is not spinning, the checks you can safely perform, and when to book a washer repair with RapidFix Kenya.

1. Unbalanced or Overloaded Drum

Modern washers have sensors that prevent spinning if the drum is unbalanced. A single heavy item, like a duvet or blanket, can throw off the weight distribution. Similarly, overloading the machine prevents the drum from reaching spin speed.

  • Remove some items and redistribute the load evenly
  • Run a spin-only cycle to test
  • Always follow the manufacturer's load capacity guidelines

2. Broken Drive Belt

Front-loading washing machines use a rubber belt to connect the motor to the drum. If the belt snaps or slips off, the drum will not spin regardless of motor activity. You may hear the motor running but see no movement.

3. Faulty Door Lock or Switch

Washing machines will not spin if the door is not securely latched. A worn door lock assembly can fail to signal the control board that the door is closed, cutting power to the spin cycle.

4. Clogged Drain Pump or Filter

If water remains in the drum after the wash cycle, the machine may lock the spin cycle to prevent overflow. A clogged drain pump, filter, or kinked drain hose traps water inside.

  • Check the drain filter at the bottom front of the machine
  • Remove coins, hair, and lint buildup
  • Inspect the drain hose for kinks or blockages

5. Failed Motor or Capacitor

If the motor has burned out or the start capacitor is weak, the drum may not reach spin speed. A humming noise without spinning is a classic sign of motor trouble. This requires professional washer repair.

People Also Ask: Washing Machine Not Spinning

Can I fix a washing machine that will not spin?

Yes, if the issue is a clogged filter or unbalanced load. For motor, belt, or control board failures, professional repair is safer and more cost-effective than replacement.

How much does washing machine spin repair cost?

Spin cycle repairs in Nairobi typically cost between KSh 2,000 and KSh 7,000, depending on whether the drive belt, drain pump, or motor needs replacement.

Why does my washing machine spin sometimes but not always?

Intermittent spinning usually indicates a failing door lock, worn carbon brushes in the motor, or an intermittent sensor fault. These issues worsen over time and should be addressed promptly.
