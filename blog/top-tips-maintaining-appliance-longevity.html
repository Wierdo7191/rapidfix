Top Tips for Maintaining Your Appliance's Longevity

8 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

Appliances are among the biggest investments in a Kenyan home. A quality fridge, washing machine, or oven can cost tens of thousands of shillings, yet many homeowners treat them as disposable items when they break down. The truth is, most appliance failures are preventable with simple, routine care. By following a few appliance care tips, you can extend the life of your devices by years and avoid costly repair bills.

At RapidFix Kenya, we have seen thousands of appliances that failed prematurely due to neglect. In this guide, we share our top tips for maintaining your appliances and keeping them running efficiently.

General Maintenance Rules for All Appliances

Before diving into appliance-specific advice, here are universal habits that apply to every device in your home:

  1. Read the manual. Manufacturer guidelines are the best source for care instructions unique to your model.
  2. Keep appliances clean. Dust, lint, and debris cause overheating and strain on motors.
  3. Ensure proper ventilation. Never push appliances flush against walls; leave space for airflow.
  4. Use the right settings. Overloading washing machines, overstuffing fridges, and using high heat unnecessarily all shorten lifespan.
  5. Schedule annual service. A professional checkup catches worn parts before they fail.

Refrigerator Maintenance

Your fridge runs 24 hours a day, making it the most vulnerable to wear. Extend its life with these steps:

  • Vacuum condenser coils every three to four months. Dust buildup forces the compressor to work harder and can cause overheating.
  • Check door seals monthly. Clean gaskets with warm soapy water and replace if cracked.
  • Keep the interior organized to avoid blocking cold air vents.
  • Defrost manual freezers before ice exceeds 5 millimeters.
  • Replace water filters on schedule to maintain water quality and ice maker flow.

Washing Machine Care

Washing machines endure constant vibration, water exposure, and detergent buildup. Protect yours with these habits:

  • Clean the drum monthly by running an empty hot cycle with vinegar or a washing machine cleaner.
  • Leave the door open after cycles to air dry and prevent mold.
  • Empty pockets before loading; coins and buttons damage the drum and drain pump.
  • Check the filter quarterly and remove lint, hair, and debris.
  • Use the correct detergent for your machine type and water hardness.

Oven and Cooker Maintenance

Ovens accumulate grease, food spills, and burner grime that affect performance and safety:

  • Clean spills immediately to prevent carbon buildup on heating elements.
  • Remove and soak burner caps weekly to keep gas flow unobstructed.
  • Inspect the door seal for cracks that let heat escape and waste energy.
  • Run self-cleaning cycles if available, but avoid doing so more than a few times per year, as extreme heat can damage sensors.
  • Calibrate the thermostat annually with an oven thermometer to ensure accurate temperatures.

Microwave Maintenance

Microwaves are low-maintenance but not zero-maintenance:

  • Wipe the interior weekly with a damp cloth to remove food splatters that can absorb microwave energy and damage the interior coating.
  • Cover food while heating to prevent explosive messes that damage the turntable and waveguide cover.
  • Run a vinegar steam clean monthly: heat a bowl of water and vinegar for three minutes, then wipe down.

TV and Electronics Care

Electronics fail from heat and dust more than age:

  • Dust vents and ports quarterly with a soft brush or compressed air.
  • Provide clearance behind TVs and sound systems for heat dissipation.
  • Use surge protectors to guard against voltage spikes common in Nairobi.
  • Avoid placing electronics near windows where sunlight and humidity accelerate wear.

People Also Ask: Appliance Maintenance

How often should I service my appliances?

Major appliances benefit from professional servicing once a year. Washing machines and dishwashers may need attention every six months in hard-water areas like parts of Nairobi.

Can I clean fridge coils myself?

Yes. Pull the fridge away from the wall, unplug it, and vacuum the coils with a brush attachment. Wear a mask to avoid inhaling dust, and work carefully to avoid damaging the coils.

What is the average lifespan of home appliances?

In Kenyan households, refrigerators last 10-15 years, washing machines 8-12 years, ovens 10-18 years, and microwaves 6-10 years. Regular maintenance can push these numbers higher.
