Top 5 Reasons Your Fridge is Not Cooling Enough

7 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

Opening your refrigerator to find lukewarm milk or soft vegetables is a frustrating discovery. When your fridge is not cooling enough, it is more than an inconvenience; it is a race against time before your groceries spoil and your energy bill spikes. Many homeowners in Nairobi assume the compressor has failed and start shopping for a new unit, but the real issue is often far simpler.

In this guide, we cover the top five reasons your refrigerator is struggling to stay cold, what you can check yourself, and when to call in the professionals for fridge repair in Nairobi.

1. Dirty Condenser Coils

Condenser coils are usually located at the back or underneath your fridge. Their job is to release heat from the interior. When covered in dust, pet hair, or Nairobi dust, they cannot dissipate heat efficiently, forcing the compressor to work overtime.

  • Pull the fridge away from the wall carefully
  • Vacuum the coils with a brush attachment
  • Clean every 3-6 months in dusty environments

Dirty coils are the single most common cause of fridge not cooling issues in Kenya.

2. Blocked Air Vents

Modern frost-free fridges circulate cold air through vents inside the compartment. If these vents are blocked by stacked containers or food packaging, cold air cannot reach all areas. Check that no items are touching the back walls of your fridge or freezer.

3. Faulty Door Seals (Gaskets)

A damaged or dirty door seal allows warm air to enter, making the compressor run constantly. Test your seal by closing a banknote in the door; if it slides out easily, the gasket needs replacement. Clean seals with warm soapy water and replace if cracked.

4. Low Refrigerant or Compressor Issues

If the fridge runs constantly but does not get cold, you may have a refrigerant leak or a failing compressor. These are serious issues that require professional refrigerator repair. Look for these warning signs:

  • Compressor running non-stop with no cooling
  • Oil spots near the compressor (leak indicator)
  • Unusual clicking sounds from the back

5. Malfunctioning Thermostat or Control Board

The thermostat tells your fridge how cold to run. If it is broken, the compressor may never kick on, or it may run indefinitely without reaching temperature. On digital fridges, a faulty control board can also disrupt cooling cycles.

People Also Ask: Fridge Not Cooling

Why is my fridge running but not getting cold?

This usually indicates dirty condenser coils, a failed compressor, or low refrigerant. Start by cleaning the coils at the back of the unit. If the problem persists, the compressor may need professional attention.

How often should I clean my fridge coils?

In Nairobi's dusty climate, clean your condenser coils every three to four months. This simple maintenance task can extend your fridge's lifespan by several years and reduce energy costs by up to 15 percent.

Can a fridge stop cooling suddenly?

Yes. A sudden loss of cooling often points to a failed start relay on the compressor, a tripped overload protector, or a refrigerant leak. These require immediate professional diagnosis to prevent food spoilage.
