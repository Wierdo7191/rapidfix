Why is My TV Screen Glitching? Common Causes and Fixes

6 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

There is nothing more frustrating than settling in for a movie night only to see your TV screen glitching, flickering, or displaying strange lines. Whether you are watching your favorite series or catching up on the news, visual disruptions can ruin the experience and signal deeper hardware problems. Understanding the root cause of TV display issues is the first step toward deciding whether a simple fix will work or if you need professional TV repair in Nairobi.

In this guide, we walk through the most common reasons why your TV is flickering or showing glitchy visuals, what you can safely check at home, and when it is time to call the experts at RapidFix Kenya.

1. Loose or Damaged HDMI and Cable Connections

One of the most overlooked causes of TV screen glitching is a loose cable. HDMI, coaxial, and component cables can wiggle free over time, especially on wall-mounted TVs where ports are under constant stress.

  • Unplug and firmly reseat all HDMI and AV cables
  • Inspect cable tips for bent pins or corrosion
  • Try a different HDMI port on the TV
  • Swap cables with known-working ones to rule out damage

2. Faulty or Aging Backlight Strips

Modern LCD and LED TVs rely on backlight strips behind the panel. When these begin to fail, you may notice dim corners, flickering brightness, or uneven lighting. Backlight failure is one of the most common TV display issues we encounter at RapidFix Kenya.

Signs of backlight problems include:

  • Screen is dark but audio works
  • Image is visible when shining a flashlight on the screen
  • Flickering that worsens in low-light conditions

3. Graphic Driver or Software Glitches

Smart TVs run on complex firmware, and bugs in the operating system can cause visual artifacts, color shifts, or screen freezing. Before assuming hardware failure, try these software steps:

  1. Power cycle the TV: unplug for 60 seconds, then restart
  2. Check for firmware updates in Settings
  3. Perform a factory reset if issues persist
  4. Disconnect external devices to rule out interference

4. Failing T-Con Board (Timing Control)

The T-Con board controls the signals sent to individual pixels on your TV screen. When it fails, you may see vertical or horizontal lines, partial images, or complete screen splitting. This is a hardware issue that requires professional TV screen repair.

5. Damaged LCD or OLED Panel

Physical damage to the panel itself is the most serious cause of glitching. Whether from an impact, pressure crack, or manufacturing defect, a damaged panel usually requires full replacement. At RapidFix Kenya, we assess whether panel replacement is cost-effective compared to a new TV.

People Also Ask: TV Screen Glitching

Why does my TV screen flicker only on certain channels?

This usually points to a signal or broadcast issue rather than a TV fault. Try switching inputs or connecting a different device to confirm whether the TV or the source is causing the flicker.

Can a power surge cause TV screen glitching?

Yes. Voltage spikes can damage the T-Con board, backlight driver, or main board. If glitching started after a power outage, have the power supply inspected immediately.

Is it worth repairing a glitching TV screen?

If the panel is intact, repairs typically cost KSh 3,500-7,000, which is far less than a new TV. Panel replacement is only recommended for premium models.
