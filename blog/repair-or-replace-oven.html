Should You Repair or Replace Your Oven?

7 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

When your cooker or oven stops working, the first question is rarely "why" -- it is "should I fix it or buy a new one?" Ovens are expensive appliances, and the decision between oven repair and replacement can feel overwhelming. The right choice depends on the age of the unit, the severity of the fault, the repair cost, and the efficiency of newer models.

At RapidFix Kenya, we believe in honest advice. This guide provides a simple framework to help you decide whether to repair or replace your oven, saving you money and stress.

Factor 1: The Age of Your Oven

The average lifespan of an oven in a Kenyan household is 10 to 18 years, depending on brand, usage, and maintenance. If your oven is under seven years old, repair is almost always the better financial choice. If it is older than twelve years and has had multiple repairs already, replacement may be more sensible.

Factor 2: The Cost of Repair vs. Replacement

A good rule of thumb is the 50 percent rule: if the repair cost exceeds 50 percent of the price of a comparable new oven, replacement is usually the better investment. However, this rule works best when you also consider installation costs, gas line modifications, and cabinet fitting.

  • Minor repairs (thermostat, sensor, door seal): KSh 2,000-4,500
  • Moderate repairs (heating element, fan motor): KSh 3,500-6,500
  • Major repairs (control board, compressor): KSh 7,000-15,000+
  • New mid-range oven: KSh 35,000-80,000

Factor 3: Energy Efficiency

Older ovens are far less efficient than modern models. An oven manufactured before 2015 may consume 20 to 30 percent more energy than a newer inverter or convection model. If you are facing a major repair on an old oven, consider the long-term savings on electricity or gas bills from an efficient replacement.

Factor 4: Availability of Spare Parts

For older or discontinued brands, finding genuine spare parts can be difficult and expensive. At RapidFix Kenya, we stock common parts for popular brands including Whirlpool, Samsung, LG, and Ariston. If parts are unavailable or excessively costly, replacement becomes the practical option.

Factor 5: Safety Concerns

Gas leaks, electrical faults, and cracked oven doors are safety issues that should never be ignored. If your oven poses any risk to your family, replacement may be the only responsible choice, regardless of cost.

Decision Table: Repair or Replace?

Scenario Age of Oven Repair Cost Recommendation
Faulty heating element Under 7 years KSh 2,500-5,000 Repair
Failed control board Over 12 years KSh 10,000+ Replace
Broken door glass Any age KSh 3,000-6,000 Repair
Gas valve failure Over 10 years KSh 8,000+ Consider replacement
Multiple past repairs Over 8 years Recurring Replace

When to Get a Free Quote First

Before deciding, get a professional diagnosis. A technician can tell you exactly what is wrong, quote the repair cost, and advise on parts availability. At RapidFix Kenya, we provide free, no-obligation quotes so you can make an informed decision.

Pro Tip: Keep records of all past repairs. If your oven has required more than two major repairs in five years, replacement is likely the smarter long-term choice.

People Also Ask: Repair or Replace Oven

Is it worth repairing a 15-year-old oven?

It depends on the repair cost. If the fix is under KSh 5,000 and the oven is in good structural condition, it may be worth it. For major repairs exceeding KSh 10,000, replacement is usually more economical.

What is the average cost of oven repair in Kenya?

Oven repairs typically range from KSh 2,000 for simple fixes to KSh 15,000 for complex electrical or gas work. Our technicians provide transparent quotes before starting any work.

How long do ovens last?

With proper maintenance, electric ovens last 10 to 18 years, while gas ovens typically last 10 to 15 years. Regular cleaning and annual calibration checks extend their lifespan significantly.
