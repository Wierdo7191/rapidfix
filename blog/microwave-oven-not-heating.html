Microwave Oven Not Heating? Here's Why

6 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

You press start, the plate spins, the light comes on, and the timer counts down. But when you open the door, your food is still ice cold. A microwave not heating is one of the most common complaints we hear at RapidFix Kenya, and while it can be alarming, the cause is often diagnosable. In this guide, we explain why your microwave runs but does not heat, what you should never attempt at home, and how our technicians restore your appliance safely.

How a Microwave Heats Food

Understanding the basics helps you appreciate why this fault is tricky. Inside every microwave, a component called the magnetron converts electrical energy into microwave radiation. This radiation agitates water molecules in your food, generating heat from the inside out. If the magnetron fails, the turntable and lights will still work, but nothing gets warm.

Top Causes of a Microwave Not Heating

1. Failed Magnetron

The magnetron is the heart of your microwave. Over time, it can burn out due to age, voltage spikes, or food arcing inside the cavity. A failed magnetron means zero heating power. This is the most common cause of microwave fault diagnosis calls in Nairobi.

2. Broken High-Voltage Diode

The high-voltage diode works with the capacitor to power the magnetron. When it fails, the magnetron receives insufficient energy to generate heat. Symptoms include a humming sound without heating and, occasionally, a burning smell.

3. Faulty Capacitor

Microwave capacitors store massive electrical charges. A failing capacitor can cause the microwave to run briefly then shut down, or heat poorly. Never attempt to test or replace a microwave capacitor yourself; it can deliver a lethal shock even when unplugged.

4. Defective Door Switch

Modern microwaves have multiple safety switches that confirm the door is closed before allowing the magnetron to fire. If one of these switches fails, the microwave will run the turntable and light but cut power to the magnetron.

Safety Warning: Do Not DIY Microwave Repairs

Microwave ovens contain high-voltage components that remain charged long after unplugging. Capacitors can deliver shocks exceeding 2,000 volts, and the magnetron contains beryllium oxide, which is hazardous if the casing cracks. Always unplug the unit and call a certified technician for microwave repair.

Most microwave repairs cost between KSh 2,000 and KSh 6,500 in Kenya, which is significantly less than buying a new unit. If your microwave is less than seven years old and the exterior is in good condition, repair is almost always the smarter financial choice. People Also Ask: Microwave Not Heating

Can I test my microwave magnetron at home?

No. Magnetron testing requires specialized tools and carries serious electrical risk. A technician can test it safely in minutes and quote you a fair price.

Why does my microwave sometimes heat and sometimes not?

Intermittent heating usually points to a failing capacitor, diode, or door switch. These components degrade over time, causing inconsistent performance before total failure.

How long do microwave magnetrons last?

With normal home use, a magnetron lasts six to ten years. Frequent heavy use, voltage surges, and food spills inside the cavity can shorten its lifespan significantly.

Pro Tip: Never run a microwave empty. Operating without food or liquid inside can cause the magnetron to overheat and fail prematurely.
