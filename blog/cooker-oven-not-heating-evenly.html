Is Your Cooker/Oven Not Heating Evenly?

6 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

There is nothing more frustrating than following a recipe to the letter, only to pull out a tray of cookies that are burnt on one side and doughy on the other. If your cooker or oven is not heating evenly, you may be tempted to blame the recipe, but the real culprit is often the appliance itself. Uneven heating is a common oven issue that stems from failing components, poor calibration, or blocked air circulation.

In this guide, we explain why your oven is not heating evenly, what you can check at home, and when to schedule cooker repair with RapidFix Kenya.

1. Faulty Heating Elements

Most electric ovens have two elements: one at the bottom for baking and one at the top for grilling or broiling. If one element burns out or develops a hot spot, heat distribution becomes uneven. Signs of element failure include:

  • Visible cracks or blisters on the element surface
  • One side of the oven cooking faster than the other
  • Element not glowing red when the oven is on

2. Defective Temperature Sensor or Thermostat

The temperature sensor monitors internal heat and signals the control board to cycle elements on and off. A drifting sensor can cause the oven to run hotter or cooler than the dial or digital display indicates, leading to uneven results.

  • Use an oven thermometer to compare actual vs. set temperature
  • If the reading is off by more than 10 degrees, the sensor needs replacement
  • Avoid leaning on the temperature sensor; it is fragile

3. Poor Rack Position and Airflow

Sometimes the issue is not mechanical but practical. Placing a tray on the lowest rack blocks bottom heat, while crowding the oven blocks convection fans. For even heating:

  • Leave at least 5 centimeters of space around each pan
  • Use the middle rack for most baking
  • Rotate trays halfway through cooking

4. Convection Fan or Motor Failure

Fan-assisted ovens rely on a motor to circulate hot air. If the fan is broken or obstructed, heat stays trapped at the top or bottom, creating hot and cold zones. Listen for the fan; if it is silent or rattling, it may need replacement.

5. Calibration Drift

Over time, oven thermostats can drift from factory settings. What the dial says is 180 degrees Celsius may actually be 200 degrees. Calibration adjustment is a simple fix that restores precise temperature control.

People Also Ask: Oven Heating Evenly

Why does my oven burn food on the bottom?

Bottom burning usually means the bottom heating element is running too hot or the thermostat is miscalibrated. Check the element for blistering and test the oven temperature with a standalone thermometer.

Can I replace an oven heating element myself?

While element replacement is mechanically simple, it requires disconnecting high-voltage power. If you are not comfortable working with appliance electricity, hire a professional to avoid shock or fire risk.
