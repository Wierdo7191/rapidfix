Washing Machine Not Draining: A Quick Fix Guide

6 min read By RapidFix Kenya Technical Team August 18, 2026

There is nothing worse than returning to your washing machine at the end of a cycle and finding it still full of soapy water. A washer not draining is a common household problem, and while it can seem alarming, the cause is often something you can check and fix yourself in minutes. In this quick fix guide, we show you how to inspect the filter and drain hose safely, and when to call in the experts for washer drain pump replacement.

Safety First

Before inspecting any part of your washing machine, unplug it from the wall. Never put your hands inside the drum while the machine is connected to power. If water is still hot from a recent wash cycle, allow it to cool before proceeding.

Step 1: Check the Drain Filter

Nearly all front-loading washing machines have a drain filter located at the bottom front of the unit, behind a small access panel. This filter catches coins, hair, lint, and small objects before they reach the drain pump.

  1. Place a shallow tray and towel on the floor in front of the machine
  2. Open the filter access panel slowly to release any trapped water
  3. Unscrew the filter counter-clockwise and pull it out
  4. Remove any debris: coins, hair, bobby pins, or lint
  5. Run water through the filter to ensure holes are clear
  6. Reinsert the filter and close the panel

Step 2: Inspect the Drain Hose

The drain hose carries water from the machine to the sink or standpipe. If it is kinked, crushed, or blocked, water cannot escape.

  • Check for kinks or twists along the entire length of the hose
  • Detach the hose from the standpipe and blow through it to check for blockages
  • Ensure the hose is not pushed too far down the drain, creating an airlock
  • Reattach securely and run a drain-only cycle to test

Step 3: Test the Drain Pump

If the filter and hose are clear but water still does not drain, the drain pump may be jammed or burned out. You can often hear the pump humming during a drain cycle. If it is silent or making a grinding noise, it likely needs replacement.

Drain pump replacement requires accessing the bottom or back of the machine, removing electrical connections, and installing a new pump. This is a job for a professional clothes washer repair technician.

Step 4: Check the Door Lock

Modern washing machines will not drain unless the door lock signals that the door is fully closed. A worn door lock can prevent the drain cycle from starting even when everything else is working.

People Also Ask: Washing Machine Not Draining

How do you unblock a washing machine drain?

Start by cleaning the drain filter at the bottom front of the machine. If that does not work, check the drain hose for kinks or blockages. For pump or motor failure, call a professional.

Can I run my washing machine if it will not drain?

No. Running a washing machine with standing water can damage the motor, cause mold growth, and flood your laundry area. Always resolve draining issues before starting a new cycle.
